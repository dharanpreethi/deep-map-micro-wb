Website

Designed and built by Aditya Kumar and Simanta Nandi, with input from Shanmugapriya T. and Simran Bhimjyani. The 2026 redesign (the 3D map, icons, soundscapes, accessibility and translation tools) was developed through Vibe Coding.

Sound & music

The site has six original soundscapes: the main theme plus a lake, pond, kund, canal and river scene. Your browser synthesises them live with the Web Audio API, so no recordings or third-party audio files are used. The instrument voices are modelled on the tanpura, bansuri, santoor, ektara, dhol, ghanta (temple bell) and shankh. The melodies move through Raag Bhupali, Durga, Megh and Malkauns from the Hindustani tradition, and the waves, rain, frogs, crickets and koel calls are synthesised too. Sound stays off until you switch it on with the speaker button. The sound design was made for this project through Vibe Coding and is released with the site under CC BY-NC 4.0.
