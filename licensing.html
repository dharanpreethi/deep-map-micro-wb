The four licence elements

What CC BY-NC 4.0 means here

Required

Attribution (BY)

Credit must be given to the Deep Mapping of Micro Water Bodies project (Shanmugapriya T., Simran Bhimjyani, Simanta Nandi and Aditya Kumar) in the manner shown on the citation page. Do not imply endorsement. Include the licence notice and a link to this archive where possible.

Not required

ShareAlike

Under CC BY-NC 4.0 (as distinct from CC BY-NC-SA), derivative works do not need to carry the same licence. Adaptations may be licensed under different terms, enabling maximum reuse in academic and community contexts, provided the use remains non-commercial.

Not permitted

Commercial use

Commercial use of this archive's content is not permitted under CC BY-NC 4.0. This is a non-commercial academic initiative dedicated to documenting the memory of India's micro water bodies. For commercial licensing enquiries, please write to shanmugapriya@iitism.ac.in.

Permitted

Adaptation

You may remix, transform and build upon the material. Academic projects, digital humanities initiatives and community archives are encouraged to adapt this dataset, methodology and framework for their own regional applications.

Community knowledge

CC BY-NC 4.0 and community knowledge — understanding the tension

Open licences permit broad reuse, while the communities who keep a water body's rituals, songs and stories have a right to say how that knowledge circulates. This archive holds the two together as follows:

  • The CC BY-NC 4.0 licence applies to the project's own scholarly apparatus: the layer write-ups, methodology, code, icons, illustrations, soundscapes and documentation.
  • Linked sources, quoted texts and photographs remain the property of their creators and are not relicensed by this project. Follow each source's own terms when you reuse it.
  • Sacred sites and ritual knowledge belong to the communities who keep them. Reuse them with care, and credit those communities as well as this archive.
  • Communities, creators and photographers may ask for any content to be corrected, credited or removed. We respond to every request — write to us.
  • Scraping this archive to train commercial AI systems falls outside the non-commercial terms of the licence and is not permitted.

For further reading on Creative Commons licensing, visit creativecommons.org.

Sound & music

Six original generative soundscapes — the site theme and the lake, pond, kund, canal and river scenes — are synthesised live in your browser with the Web Audio API. No recorded samples or third-party audio files are used. The instrument voices are modelled on the tanpura, bansuri, santoor, ektara, dhol, ghanta (temple bell) and shankh; melodies are improvised in Raag Bhupali, Durga, Megh and Malkauns from the Hindustani tradition; waves, rain, frogs, crickets and koel calls are synthesised too. Sound design created for this project with AI assistance (Claude by Anthropic), released with the site under CC BY-NC 4.0.
