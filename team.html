Lead Researcher

Shanmugapriya T.

Assistant Professor (Digital Humanities), Department of Humanities and Social Sciences, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad

Shanmugapriya T. is an Assistant Professor (Digital Humanities) in the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences, Indian Institute of Technology (Indian School of Mines) Dhanbad. She was a Postdoctoral Scholar at the Department of Historical and Cultural Studies, University of Toronto Scarborough, Canada, from 2022 to 2024, and an AHRC Postdoctoral Research Associate at Lancaster University, UK, from 2020 to 2021. She was a SPARC Visiting Researcher and a British Academy Visiting Fellow at the Department of History, Lancaster University, in 2019 and 2025.

Her research and teaching interests have an interdisciplinary focus on digital humanities, digital environmental humanities and digital literature. Shanmu earned her Ph.D. in Indian English Literature and Digital Humanities at the Indian Institute of Technology Indore, India. She currently serves as Secretary of DHARTI and as a member of the Board of Directors of the Electronic Literature Organization.

Simran Bhimjyani

Doctoral Scholar, Department of Humanities and Social Sciences, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad · MA English, Banaras Hindu University

Simran Bhimjyani is a Doctoral Scholar in the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, working in Digital Environmental Humanities. Her research applies eco-computational methods to Indian English drama. Her work on Indian eco-electronic literature has appeared in the New Review of Hypermedia and Multimedia (2025). She is a HASTAC Scholar (2025–2027), serves on the Advisory Committee of the Global Digital Humanities Symposium (2026, 2027) and contributes to The Digital Orientalist.

Simanta Nandi

MA, Digital Humanities and Social Sciences, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad · BA Comparative Literature, Jadavpur University

Simanta Nandi is an MA student in Digital Humanities and Social Sciences at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Dhanbad. He recently completed the Summer Research Internship Program 2026 of the Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology (IIST), Thiruvananthapuram, working on preserving cultural narratives. He completed his BA in Comparative Literature at Jadavpur University, specialising in translation through the Centre for Translation of Indian Literature (CENTIL).

He participated in the 15-day UNESCO World Heritage Education Program at Visva-Bharati and a British Library archival digitisation workshop in collaboration with the Institute of Language Studies and Research (ILSR). His article “Impact of Popular Culture and Media on Literary Reception in the Northeast of India” appears in Re-imagining Northeast Writing and Narratives: Language, Culture and Border Identities (Jamia Millia Islamia, 2024). His research focuses on digital archiving, cultural heritage, AI for humanities, electronic literature and digital memory studies, and he was recognised by Oxford University Press (OUP India) in 2021 for literary excellence.

Aditya Kumar

MA, Digital Humanities and Social Sciences, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad · B.Tech EEE, SRM Institute of Science and Technology

Aditya Kumar is a postgraduate student in Digital Humanities and Social Sciences at the Indian Institute of Technology (Indian School of Mines), Dhanbad. He holds a B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai. His interdisciplinary path reflects a move from engineering to the humanities, with experience across industry and public institutions including HighRadius, JSW Steel and the National Human Rights Commission. He has also taken part in the Public Policy in Practice workshop organised by IIT Tirupati and CRISP.

His research interests lie at the intersection of technology and the humanities, with a particular focus on AI for humanities, digital cultures and computational approaches to cultural and social inquiry. He is especially interested in how technological systems can be critically integrated with humanistic perspectives to address contemporary societal challenges.
